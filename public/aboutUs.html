About NeoPapyrus

Prajwal Niraula

Prajwal started NeoPapyrus to make technical subjects easier to explore. He earned his PhD in Earth, Atmospheric, and Planetary Sciences at MIT and studies what starlight can tell us about the atmospheres of exoplanets. Read his research.

Education in Nepal

Prajwal wants to devote more time to science education in Nepal. He is interested in building resources that students can explore at their own pace and teachers can use alongside their lessons.
