NeoPapyrus is a library of interactive science and mathematics books. Start with the main explanation, then use the reading settings to reveal more detail or hide equations as you read.

Prajwal Niraula

Prajwal started NeoPapyrus to make technical subjects easier to explore. He earned his PhD in Earth, Atmospheric, and Planetary Sciences at MIT and studies what starlight can tell us about the atmospheres of exoplanets.

Education in Nepal

Prajwal wants to devote more time to science education in Nepal. He is interested in building resources that students can explore at their own pace and teachers can use alongside their lessons.
